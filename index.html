How to complete production order and record goods movements in SAP

Enter TCode “MD61” and press the “Enter” key on your keyboard to proceed to the next screen.

1/64

In the window, add the Finished Goods Material Name.

2/64

Go to the “Requirement Plan“ input field and type a name for the plan and press the “Enter” key again to advance to the following screen.

3/64

Add the planned Finished goods amount under the relevant cell for the time period.

4/64
Click to confirm your selection or action at this step.
5/64

Type the Tcode “MD02” and press enter.

6/64

Click on the “Search Help” button on “Create Purchase Req.”

7/64

Select “Planned Orders” from the selection.

8/64

Click on the “Search Help” button on“Schedule Items”

9/64

Press the “Enter” key 2 times to continue advance to the next page.

10/64
Click the “Continue” button to confirm your action and proceed.
11/64

MRP has run now.Scroll down to see the full document information.

12/64

Enter TCode “MD04” and click enter.

13/64

Type the “Material“ and “Plant Number“ press the “Enter” key to advance.

14/64

A planned order has been created as shown.

15/64

Double-click on the planned order to select and enter its detailed view.

16/64

Click the “-> Prod.Ord.” button to covert to a Production Order.

17/64

Click “Save” button to save and confirm.

18/64

Next, click the “Refresh” button to update the current information displayed on the screen.

19/64

As shown the order it is converted into a “Production Order.”
Double click on the production order. Also save the “Order Number” for later use.

20/64

It'll redirect to the “Production Order Create:Header” Screen.
Click the “Release Order” button to release the production order .

21/64

Click save to confirm.

22/64

Enter TCode “CO11N” and press enter to go to Order Operations Confirmation.

23/64

Add the relevant Production Order Number and Operation.

24/64

Press the Enter key to advance in the process.

25/64

As shown, information will be displayed. Click the “Goods Movements” button to access the section related to inventory changes or transactions.

26/64

Click on the relevant cell in-front of each FG/SFG/Raw material to add the relevant storage location.

27/64

After entering all click “Save” to confirm.

28/64

If the process is successful, a message “Confirmation saved (Goods movements: xxx, failed: 0)” will be shown at the bottom.

29/64

Add the relevant Production Order Number and next Operation.

30/64

As shown, information will be displayed. Click the “Goods Movements” button to access the section related to inventory changes or transactions.

31/64

Click on the relevant cell in-front of each SFG/Raw material to add the relevant storage location.

32/64

Click on “Save” to save and confirm.

33/64

Continue to do this steps until all operations are covered.

34/64

Enter “MD04” and press enter to view the order.

35/64

Enter the Material and press enter.

36/64

As shown, the order is now waiting for Quality Inspection.

37/64

Enter TCode “QA32” and press enter.

38/64

Enter the Finished Good Material Name in “Material” and press enter.

39/64

Click to select the row, from which the relevant order will be selected.

40/64

Next, click the “Results” button.

41/64

Enter the result and press enter.

42/64

A confirmation popup will open and mark the acceptance/rejection and click “OK”.

43/64

Click “Save” to save and confirm.

44/64

Click the “Usage decision (UD)” button.

45/64

Enter the information to mark the “Usage Decision” and press enter.

46/64

A popup will display to select the confirmation.Click the “Confirmation” drop down.

47/64

Select the “Accept” button.

48/64

Press the “Confirm” button at the bottom.

49/64

Click at the “Inspection to Stock” tab.

50/64

Click on the text box for “Unrestricted Use” and enter the relevant stock amount.

51/64

Click the “Save” button to save and confirm.

52/64

Click the “Refresh” button to update the current page or data display, ensuring you see the latest information.

53/64

As you can see, the order has passed Quality Inspection fully which is shown in green color

54/64

Enter “CO02” and press enter.

55/64

Enter the Production Order number for ”Order“ and press enter.

56/64

Click on “Functions” on “Menu Bar”

57/64

Select “Restrict Processing” from the menu. This will apply restrictions to the processing of the current item or record.

58/64

Click to “Complete Technically” to finish the process..

59/64

Click “Save” to save and confirm.

60/64

As shown, “Order number 1003343 saved” will be shown.

61/64

Enter “MD04” and press enter.

62/64

Enter the “Production Order” for “Order“ and press enter.

63/64

As shown, the Finished Goods are received into stock.
